# Analytical Assumptions

1. **Fleet Longevity**: Average annual mileage of private Ethiopian EVs is assumed to be 18,000 km; commercial fleet vehicles (ride-hailing / taxi) average 45,000 km annually.
2. **Road Surface Deterioration Factor**: Due to road surface roughness and speed bumps in Addis Ababa, suspension wear intervals are modeled at 50% of manufacturer baseline intervals.
3. **Grid Quality Impact**: Unregulated residential voltage fluctuations (±20% surges) significantly elevate On-Board Charger (OBC) capacitor failure rates.
4. **Exchange Rate Baseline**: Calculations assume prevailing commercial bank foreign exchange rates.
