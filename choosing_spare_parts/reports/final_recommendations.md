# Ethiopia EV Spare Parts: Final Import & Supply Recommendations

## Executive Summary
Following the complete ban on internal combustion engine (ICE) passenger cars enacted by the Ministry of Transport and Logistics (MoTL), Ethiopia's EV fleet has expanded to over 100,000 units, dominated by **BYD Seagull (38%)**, **BYD Yuan Up (22%)**, and **Changan Benben E-Star (18%)**.

However, aftermarket support and component availability present critical operational bottlenecks.

---

## Top 5 Component Import Priorities

1. **Front Suspension Struts & Shock Absorbers (BYD Seagull / Changan Benben)**:
   * *Rationale*: Rapid premature wear caused by heavy battery curb weight combined with Addis Ababa road potholes.
   * *Recommendation*: Establish immediate bulk sea-freight procurement of reinforced heavy-duty aftermarket struts.
2. **On-Board Chargers (OBC - 3.3kW & 6.6kW)**:
   * *Rationale*: Sensitive to Ethiopian grid voltage spikes and ungrounded residential outlets, causing frequent internal power FET blowouts.
   * *Recommendation*: Import modular replacement boards and partner with local electronics repair hubs in Kera/Saris.
3. **High-Voltage Electric AC Compressors**:
   * *Rationale*: Essential not only for cabin comfort but for battery thermal chiller loops.
   * *Recommendation*: Scheduled procurement for BYD e-Platform 3.0 vehicles.
4. **GB/T to CCS2 / Type 2 Fast Charge Adapters**:
   * *Rationale*: Facilitates public charging inter-compatibility.
5. **Auxiliary DC-DC Converters (Volkswagen ID.4 / ID.6)**:
   * *Rationale*: High failure rate with severe local scarcity (18+ day lead times).

---

## Policy & Supply Chain Recommendations
* **Customs Green Channel**: Fast-track HS Code `8504.40` (inverters/chargers) and `8507.60` (battery packs) through Modjo Dry Port.
* **Technician Certification**: Establish formal high-voltage safety and battery diagnostics training with TVET institutes and MOENCO.
